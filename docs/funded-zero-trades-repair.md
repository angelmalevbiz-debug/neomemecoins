# Funded PAPER candidate and observation repair

The four allocated books had no entries. A working backend and passing tests
did not establish an achieved trade rate or profitable strategy. The young
market screens repeatedly selected candidates whose modeled fixed round-trip
cost was about 2.68–2.78%, above the unchanged 1.5% funded budget. Missing
confirmed flow was the first refusal, hiding the additional cost constraint.

This prospective change gives Momentum and Precision an additional established
liquid market branch. Their original young-market branches remain available.
Early Runner and Ultra Precision retain their existing candidate screens.

| Constraint | Established Momentum | Established Precision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pool | PumpSwap, identified SOL denomination | Same |
| Minimum age | 24 hours | 24 hours |
| Minimum liquidity | $200,000 | $300,000 |
| Five-minute price change | +0.5% to +15% | +0.5% to +8% |
| Hourly price change | 0% to +40% | 0% to +20% |
| Minimum scanner buy/sell count ratio | 1.2 | 1.5 |
| Minimum liquidity/market-cap fraction | 0.015 | 0.015 |
| Hourly volume/liquidity | 0.03 to 3 | 0.03 to 1.5 |

These are declared research screens, not thresholds derived from measured
winning outcomes. The mature branches use finite physical market observations
instead of the headline score, which includes a preference for young tokens.
Missing or conflicting inputs cannot match these branches. Scanner counts do
not replace confirmed chain evidence. Tape scheduling uses the same declared
funded universe, and its cost model only prioritizes observation.

Every funded entry still needs complete fresh 30-second exact-pool flow, at
least three swaps from two wallets, at least 1.2 times buy/sell USD, completed
fresh same-pool safety, independent price verification, and full modeled costs
within 1.5%. The initial entries were capped at $62.50 per $250 book. The
prospective V4 PAPER sizing policy raises the per-entry cap to $125 (half of
each independent book); existing positions and accounting remain unchanged.
This increases simulated dollar gains and losses alike, and actual gaps may
exceed the modeled net stop. Flow and
safety are checked against the receipt clock and rechecked before committing,
so provider delay cannot revive old evidence. New entries record their matched
branch and the separate V3 funded policy. Completed trades and existing exits
retain their original attribution and accounting.

The recorder also handles a verified PumpSwap instruction with SOL or USDC as
the onchain base and the tracked token as quote. It inverts token BUY/SELL,
values the actual cash leg and obtains token decimals from exact transaction
balance metadata. Pool, actor, both user accounts, instruction type, event time,
signer and fresh currency-reference checks remain required. Each event must
match a distinct instruction. Unsupported or incomplete observations remain
unclassified; historical classifications are not rewritten.

The collapsed strategy row explains when every current matching candidate
exceeds the model's cost limit. Aggregate counts must cover every signal;
truncated examples or unknown costs cannot establish that conclusion. A lost
backend connection displays an unconfirmed state instead of a current refusal.

The fee model remains an estimate, including its fallback assumptions about
canonical PumpSwap fee tiers. Actual noncanonical fees require authenticated
pool/configuration evidence before any lower rate could be applied. The model
does not establish executable prices or real profitability.

Synthetic eligible-loop tests prove that permitted entries can open and that
refused entries preserve money and history. They do not establish five to ten
trades per half-hour, profitability, or an 80% win rate. Those outcomes require
new real-time PAPER observations and closed trades net of costs and losses.
