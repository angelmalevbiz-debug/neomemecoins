- Holdout = the last 40% of the span by entry time (about 9.1 h); train = the first 60%.
- CI95 = bootstrap over (pair, hour) clusters of the mean $ per trade; `CI95 pair` resamples whole pairs (more conservative).
- `portfolio` = H.portfolio(holdout trades, slots=3, start $1000): trades taken, final balance, max drawdown.
- Variants: `orig` = as submitted; `rug_guard_v1` = the same with the frozen rug guard ANDed in (run(H) configs: guard ORed into their H.interim_rug_risk). Baselines are random entries in the same universe with the same exits, run in both variants.
